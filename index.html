Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 4, 2026

Local Data Storage

Aevion Pro stores business data locally on your device. This may include projects, clients, vehicles, locksmith jobs, inventory, receipts, payment records, invoices, contracts, reminders, notes, templates, and backup files.

No Developer Access

The developer does not have access to your app data. Aevion Pro does not upload your business records to a developer server.

No Tracking or Analytics

Aevion Pro does not track your activity across other apps or websites. The app does not use third-party advertising tracking or third-party analytics services.

No Data Sharing

Aevion Pro does not sell, share, or transfer your personal or business data to third parties.

User Control

You are responsible for managing and protecting your data. The app may provide backup export and import features so you can save your own backup files using Apple Files, iCloud Drive, On My iPhone, AirDrop, or other destinations available on your device.

Permissions & Data Usage

Aevion Pro only asks for permissions when a related feature is used. Permissions are not requested on launch unless required by a feature you choose to enable.

Photos

Used to select images such as payment QR codes, receipts, vehicle photos, project attachments, or client documents.

Camera

Used to take photos for receipts, documents, vehicles, inventory, or project records when you choose to capture an image.

Files

Used to export and import backup files, invoices, contracts, receipts, or other documents through the iOS Files system.

Notifications

Used for reminders related to jobs, payments, follow-ups, service schedules, inventory, and backups.

Contacts

Used only if you choose to import clients from your device contacts.

Backup Responsibility

Aevion Pro is designed as an offline-first app. Because your data is stored locally, you are responsible for keeping backups of important business information.

We recommend exporting a backup at least once per week and saving it somewhere safe, such as iCloud Drive or another secure storage location.
